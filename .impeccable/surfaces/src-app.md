# Report flow: who fixes it

Scope: the resident report flow (photo, issue, location, routed answer, report) plus home and community map. Mode: Operate. Audience: VA-08 residents on a phone, many older or reading in Spanish. Job: reach the one correct office with a ready report in under a minute. Constraints: WCAG 2.2 AA, 44px targets, words with every icon, English and Spanish, never send on the user's behalf.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The answer is a road sign pointing at the office that fixes this exact spot, shown over the resident's own photo. Refuses the category default of a white form with a blue card.

OWN-WORLD: Virginia road signage on a full-screen photo. Guide-sign green #00653b with a white inset border and arrow for every answer; white regulatory plate with the wrong office struck through; route-marker badges for levels: local blue rounded square, state white "STATE" plate, utility yellow diamond, federal black-and-white shield. Near-black ground #0c0f14, white type, Overpass throughout.

STORY: The resident sees their spot, watches three checks resolve as route markers (district, road owner, streetlight owner), sees which one decided it, reads why the obvious office is wrong, then sends the drafted report themselves.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top 84px dark band with wordmark, EN/ES and a five-segment progress bar. Photo or map fills to about 470px with a right rail of labeled Call, Copy, Map buttons. A "Checked" strip of route markers sits on the photo's lower edge. The green guide sign (office name 38px, level marker, arrow) anchors at 470px, the struck-through plate and one-line reason below, and a full-width 60px "Report to <office>" button at the bottom.
