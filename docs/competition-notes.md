# 2026 Congressional App Challenge: notes

Source: [2026 CAC Rules (PDF)](https://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026-CAC-Rules.pdf), [rules page](https://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/students/rules/). Saved Oct 21, 2026.

## Deadline
- **12:00 pm (noon) EDT, Monday, October 26, 2026.** Registration and submission. Submission can't be changed after.
- Plan to submit Sunday night.

## Eligibility
- Every student must be enrolled in middle or high school on October 26, 2026.
- Teams of up to 4. At least half must live or attend school in the district entered.
- Must be U.S. residents (no citizenship requirement).
- One app per person per year; app must have been created after October 30, 2025.
- Registration needs a personal (non-school) email, home and school addresses with 9-digit ZIP, district and Member, and a parent/guardian's name and email.

## Theme
- No required topic, platform, or language. The app must show real functionality.

## AI and originality rules
- AI tools are allowed only if **all** AI use is fully disclosed in the submission.
- AI "may only be used to support specific aspects of the project and must not constitute the entirety of the technical development."
- Students must show "significant individual contributions and technical understanding."
- Open-source libraries are allowed but must be documented.
- Judges can request the app and source code; refusing means disqualification.

## Judging criteria
1. Quality of the idea (creativity, originality)
2. Implementation (user experience, design)
3. Demonstrated excellence of coding and programming skills

Members of Congress may modify the process or use a panel.

## Submission
**Demo video, 1 to 3 minutes, public on YouTube or Vimeo.** Must include:
- Each participant's name
- App name
- Purpose in one clear sentence
- Target audience
- Tools and coding languages used
- The app working

**Written questions (similar to):**
1. Title of your app
2. The app's purpose
3. What inspired you to create it?
4. What technical difficulty did you face, and how did you address it?
5. What did you learn? Biggest takeaway?
6. What would you change in a 2.0 version?

After the deadline, every team member must complete the Exit Questionnaire individually.

## Past winners worth knowing (2025)
- **Pothole Patrol** (IN-07, Rep. André Carson): voice-activated iOS app; drivers say "Hey Patch, report a pothole." [Page](https://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/25-IN07/)
- **RoadWatch** (NJ-06, Rep. Frank Pallone): AI dashcam system that detects road hazards for drivers and local governments. [Page](https://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/25-NJ06/)
- Both led with a personal story. Full list: [2025 winners](https://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/2025-winners/)
