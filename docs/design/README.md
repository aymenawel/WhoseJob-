# Visual direction

## What the team liked (Oct 21, 2026)

- **Guide Sign**: the coloring. The answer is a green highway guide sign pointing at the office. See `liked-guide-sign.png`.
- **Full-Screen Story**: the layout. The resident's photo fills the screen, the answer sits low, and actions run down a labeled rail. See `liked-full-screen-story.png`.

The other options we reviewed are in `options-first.png` and `options-bolder.png`.

## Proposed fusion: Guide Sign × Full-Screen Story

The mockups are `fusion-sheet.png` (both screens), `fusion-streetlight.html`, and `fusion-pothole.html`.

- **Layout (from Full-Screen Story):** each step is one full screen.
  - The photo fills the top.
  - A labeled action rail (Call, Copy, Map) sits on the right.
  - A five-segment progress bar runs across the top.
  - One big primary button is at the bottom.
- **The answer (from Guide Sign):** a green guide panel with a white inset border and an arrow. It is always green: green means "this is the way to go."
- **What it checked:** a strip of route markers above the sign: "Checked VA-08 · Arlington road · Dominion pole." The marker that decided the answer has a white outline.
- **Why not the obvious office:** a white plate with the obvious office struck through ("~~Arlington County~~ not the county"), then one plain sentence.
- **Levels of government** are shown by a marker on the sign, in the way real guide signs carry route shields. Each marker has its own shape, color, and word, so it reads without color:

  | Level | Marker |
  | --- | --- |
  | Local (county or city) | Blue rounded square |
  | State (VDOT) | White rectangle with a black border, labeled "STATE" |
  | Utility (Dominion) | Yellow diamond |
  | Federal (Member of Congress) | Black-and-white shield |

- **Type:** Overpass, an open-source face based on the lettering of U.S. highway signs.

## Known gaps in the mockups

- The photos are drawn stand-ins; the app uses the resident's real photo. A no-photo version (map or plain ground) still needs designing.
- Over bright photos, the rail labels need a dark backing for contrast. The top bar already has one.
- The federal (shield) and local (blue) answer screens are not drawn yet.
